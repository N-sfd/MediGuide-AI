Fever Basics

A fever is a temporary rise in body temperature and is often part of the body's
response to infection or inflammation. Normal body temperature can vary by
person, time of day, and measurement method.

Educational materials usually explain that fever itself is a symptom, not a
diagnosis. Useful details to track include temperature readings, how long the
fever has lasted, associated symptoms, fluids taken, and medicines used for
comfort if advised.

Urgent care guidance in public education often includes very high fever,
fever with severe stiff neck, difficulty breathing, confusion, seizures, or
fever in infants according to clinician guidance.

Rest, hydration, and clinician-directed comfort measures are commonly discussed.
This information is educational and is not a treatment plan for any person.
