Cholesterol Basics

Cholesterol is a waxy substance the body needs to build cells. The body makes
cholesterol, and foods can also contribute to blood cholesterol levels.

Patient education materials often discuss LDL cholesterol, sometimes called
"bad" cholesterol, and HDL cholesterol, sometimes called "good" cholesterol.
Triglycerides are another blood fat measured with cholesterol panels.

High LDL cholesterol can contribute to plaque buildup in arteries over time.
That buildup is associated with higher risk of heart disease and stroke.

Cholesterol results are interpreted with other risk factors such as blood
pressure, smoking status, diabetes, age, and family history.

General habits discussed in cholesterol education include heart-healthy eating,
physical activity as advised, not smoking, and following clinician guidance on
medications when prescribed.

This information is educational and is not a diagnosis or treatment plan.
